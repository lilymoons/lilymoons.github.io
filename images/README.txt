Drop your image files in here, named to match what's referenced in js/data.js and index.html:

  cover.jpg              - home page hero cover
  wren.jpg                - cast avatar (square-ish, cropped to a circle)
  bram.jpg
  nell.jpg
  tidekeeper.jpg
  ep1.jpg                 - full chapter 1 image (tall, vertical scroll)
  ep1-thumb.jpg            - optional cropped thumbnail for chapter 1
  ep2.jpg / ep2-thumb.jpg
  ep3.jpg / ep3-thumb.jpg

Any of the *-thumb.jpg files can be skipped - the full chapter image
will be used and auto-cropped to fit the thumbnail square instead.
